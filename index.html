Case study 01 · Platform transformation

Building a Scalable Knowledge Platform for Customer Self-Service

I led the evaluation and transition from MindTouch to Document360 to address search and navigation pain points, improve the organization of technical information, and establish a stronger foundation for AI-powered search and customer self-service.

Customer experience · Knowledge management · AI

Making technical information easier to find

My role: documentation initiative owner and migration lead, aligning platform decisions with customer needs and business priorities.

The challenge

Customer feedback and internal stakeholder comments highlighted friction in the technical documentation portal, particularly around finding relevant information and navigating the content. The goal was to make search more effective and organize knowledge so users could reach the right sections with less effort.

What I did

  1. Defined the evaluation criteria. Turned customer feedback and internal requirements into practical use cases for assessing search, information organization, customization, integrations, and AI capabilities.
  2. Compared platforms against real workflows. Evaluated multiple authoring options against the use cases and operational needs, rather than choosing on features alone.
  3. Led the platform transition. Selected Document360 and coordinated the move of Help content to establish a more capable foundation for content discovery, navigation, and maintenance.

Value created

  • Improved search experience: The new platform delivered a noticeable improvement in search, making it easier for users to find relevant technical information.
  • Clearer information architecture: Better-organized content helped users navigate the documentation and access the appropriate sections more easily.
  • Foundation for AI-powered self-service: Established a stronger platform base for future AI-enabled knowledge discovery, with customer and internal stakeholder feedback informing ongoing improvements.

How this supports customer and business outcomes

Customer success

A more capable documentation platform gives customers a clearer route to relevant product guidance when they need to complete a task or resolve a question.

Self-service

Improved search and clearer content organization make it easier for users to discover relevant guidance and navigate directly to the sections they need.

Operational efficiency

Search and usage analytics create a practical basis for prioritizing content fixes instead of relying only on ad hoc requests.

AI readiness

The platform transition establishes a stronger foundation for structured knowledge discovery and future AI-enabled experiences; it does not by itself prove AI performance gains.

Qualitative improvements in search and navigation were observed, alongside internal stakeholder feedback. Quantified effects on search success, support volume, or customer outcomes have not yet been established.

Evidence status: The reported improvements are qualitative: better search, stronger information organization, and easier navigation to relevant sections. Next-step measures could include search-success rates, unsuccessful searches, user feedback, and documentation-related support trends.

Case study 02 · AI-assisted content operations

Operationalizing AI in Documentation Workflows

I introduced a structured, multi-agent workflow using Gemini and Jira agents to help the team analyse product changes, prepare drafts, and review content. This approach reduces repetitive effort and allows writers to focus more on technical validation, editorial judgment, and customer needs.

From manual drafting to a structured AI-assisted workflow

I used a Gemini agent to analyse product information and prepare drafts, alongside a review agent to evaluate content against its purpose, audience, style, and quality expectations. A Jira agent analyses linked issues and subtasks to help writers understand changes and prepare content for final author review.

The workflow helps writers analyse Jira stories and product changes more efficiently. This enables faster responses to documentation requests and more time for high-value editorial and strategic work, with writers retaining responsibility for technical accuracy and final quality.

1. Analyse product changes

Use structured prompts to examine product information, Jira stories, linked issues, and relevant context to identify documentation implications and user needs.

2. Develop a first draft

Guide AI-assisted drafting using product terminology, content standards, and the intended user task to create a starting point for writers.

3. Review and refine

Apply a separate review stage to assess completeness, structure, clarity, and alignment with product behaviour and documentation standards.

4. Retain human ownership

Writers validate technical details and resolve gaps with subject-matter experts. I review, edit, or write content directly when needed to maintain quality.

Approach: AI supports the workflow; it does not replace technical validation, editorial judgment, or writer accountability. Time savings have not been formally quantified.

My approach

A Practical Framework for Better Knowledge Experiences

Effective documentation starts with customer needs and continues through structured content, editorial quality, discoverability, and continuous improvement.

01 · Understand

Understand customer questions, product workflows, support cases, and business priorities before deciding what content to create or improve.

02 · Structure

Organize information around user tasks, clear information architecture, consistent terminology, and reusable knowledge patterns.

03 · Create

Develop task-focused documentation, FAQs, release notes, troubleshooting guidance, and other content that helps customers act with confidence.

04 · Validate

Partner with subject-matter experts and apply editorial review to maintain technical accuracy, completeness, clarity, and consistency.

05 · Enable discovery

Improve search, navigation, and content structure so users and AI-enabled experiences can retrieve relevant information more effectively.

06 · Improve

Use feedback, usage patterns, content age, and stakeholder input to prioritize maintenance and guide future improvements.
