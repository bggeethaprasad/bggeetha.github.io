My approach

A Practical, Customer-First Documentation Framework

I connect customer needs, product knowledge, and content operations through a repeatable approach that keeps documentation useful, accurate, discoverable, and aligned with business priorities.

1. Understand the user

Start with customer goals, common questions, support cases, and the tasks users need to complete.

2. Connect content to product

Work with Product, Engineering, and Support to understand product behaviour, identify gaps, and keep guidance accurate.

3. Design for discovery

Use clear information architecture, meaningful titles, consistent terminology, and practical examples to help readers find answers.

4. Use evidence to prioritize

Use page views, search patterns, customer feedback, and content age to identify the most valuable improvements.

5. Apply AI with governance

Use AI to support analysis and drafting, with structured review, technical validation, and human ownership of the final content.

6. Improve continuously

Review feedback and content performance, address gaps, and refine the documentation experience as products evolve.
