Case study 01 · Platform transformation

Building a Scalable Knowledge Platform for Customer Self-Service

Led the evaluation and transition from MindTouch to Document360 to improve content discoverability, establish a scalable knowledge platform, and prepare the documentation ecosystem for AI-powered search and future knowledge experiences.

The challenge

The documentation platform needed a stronger foundation for discoverability, intuitive information organization, and scalable knowledge delivery.

What I did

  • Led the evaluation and transition from MindTouch to Document360.
  • Focused on improving the organization and navigation of product knowledge.
  • Worked with stakeholders and considered feedback during the transition.
  • Established a stronger foundation for analytics, content maintenance, and future AI-enabled experiences.

Observed impact

  • Search improved noticeably on the new platform.
  • Information was organized more effectively, helping users navigate and access relevant sections more easily.
  • Internal stakeholder comments provided additional feedback on the platform experience.
Business value: A more discoverable and navigable knowledge platform, with a stronger foundation for customer self-service and AI-powered search.

Case study 02 · AI-enabled operations

Operationalizing AI in Documentation Workflows

Designed and operationalized an AI-assisted documentation workflow that supports product analysis, drafting, editorial review, and quality governance while maintaining human accountability for technical accuracy and final content quality.

The challenge

Documentation work involved substantial manual effort in analyzing product changes, preparing first drafts, and reviewing content for consistency and quality.

What I did

  • Identified opportunities to use AI across the documentation workflow.
  • Structured a workflow that uses AI to analyze Jira stories and product changes more efficiently.
  • Integrated AI-assisted drafting and editorial review into day-to-day practices.
  • Established quality expectations and retained human ownership of technical accuracy and final approval.

Outcomes

  • Reduced effort required to create first drafts.
  • Improved consistency in structure, terminology, and documentation standards.
  • Streamlined editorial reviews by producing stronger initial drafts.
  • Helped writers respond to documentation requests more efficiently.
  • Shifted more writer effort toward validation, content quality, and customer-focused design.
Business value: A structured, human-governed AI workflow that improves drafting efficiency and gives writers more capacity for high-value editorial and strategic work. Quantitative time savings have not been measured here.

Case study 03 · Content modernization

Using Customer Insights to Drive Documentation Improvement

Led a content modernization initiative that prioritized high-value documentation using usage insights and content-age assessment, improving content currency and strengthening the customer documentation experience.

The challenge

Frequently viewed documentation needed to remain current, complete, and useful as products and customer needs evolved.

What I did

  • Generated a Top Viewed Topics report.
  • Reviewed the age of highly viewed content to identify topics that might need refreshing.
  • Shared the report with Product Managers and Engineering teams.
  • Collaborated with stakeholders to identify priority topics and analyze content gaps.
  • Updated the selected topics, enhanced their visual presentation, and added use cases to all top-viewed topics.

Observed impact

  • Created a clearer, evidence-informed approach to prioritizing documentation improvements.
  • Strengthened the currency and usefulness of frequently accessed content.
  • Made the updated topics more practical through additional use cases and visual improvements.
  • Gathered encouraging early customer feedback following the migration and content refresh initiative.
Business value: A more systematic approach to content modernization that combines usage data, content age, stakeholder expertise, and customer needs.

Case study 04 · AI-ready knowledge

Building AI-Ready Knowledge Experiences

Partnered with Support and Product teams to transform customer questions, troubleshooting guidance, and best practices into structured knowledge assets that support AI-powered self-service experiences.

The challenge

AI-powered self-service depends on relevant, clear, well-structured knowledge that maps to the questions customers actually ask.

What I did

  • Analyzed common Support cases to understand recurring customer questions and product issues.
  • Collaborated with Support and Product stakeholders to identify relevant knowledge needs.
  • Rewrote existing content to make the information clearer and easier for AI-powered systems to retrieve.
  • Focused on aligning knowledge content with real customer problems and solutions.
Business value: Better-prepared knowledge assets for AI retrieval and customer self-service. This describes content readiness, not a measured improvement in AI answer accuracy or support volume.

Case study 05 · Strategic leadership

Leading Documentation as a Strategic Business Capability

As a Documentation and Knowledge Management Leader, I own the Product Documentation function and drive initiatives that improve content quality, operational maturity, customer self-service, and organizational readiness for AI-enabled experiences.

Leadership approach

I identified the opportunity to use AI within documentation workflows, developed a structured approach for adoption, trained the team on effective usage, established quality expectations, and integrated the workflow into day-to-day documentation practices.

Leadership outcomes

  • Introduced AI-assisted documentation workflows that improved drafting efficiency and enabled writers to focus more on validation and content quality.
  • Guided the team toward a more customer-focused documentation approach, shifting emphasis from feature descriptions to user goals and outcomes.
  • Strengthened collaboration across Product, Support, and Engineering teams to improve alignment between product changes and documentation priorities.
  • Established consistent documentation and review practices that improved quality, knowledge sharing, and team effectiveness.
  • Balanced documentation strategy, operational delivery, and team development while maintaining hands-on editorial leadership.
Leadership value: A more structured and customer-focused documentation function, supported by team capability building, quality expectations, and cross-functional alignment.
