Overview

I manage and develop a team of technical writers by combining delivery and workload information, documentation quality feedback, and individual observations. Through one-to-one discussions and ongoing coaching, I identify team strengths and development areas, allocate work thoughtfully, and encourage writers to take initiative, broaden their skills, and share knowledge.

1. Combined data with individual insights

I consider task delivery, workload, quality and review feedback, product knowledge, individual skills, learning progress, and documentation output. I complement available data with observations and one-to-one discussions to understand individual strengths, challenges, and development opportunities.

2. Aligned work allocation with capabilities and growth

I consider workload, task complexity, product knowledge, and individual strengths when assigning work. I encourage writers to develop new skills and take on unfamiliar challenges while balancing those opportunities with delivery commitments.

3. Encouraged initiative and ownership

I encourage team members to suggest improvements, explore better ways to communicate product information, and take ownership beyond routine assignments. Coaching and regular discussions help writers build confidence and contribute ideas to team practices.

4. Expanded content creation through videos

I create instructional videos and encourage writers to develop video-based content where visual demonstrations help explain product workflows and features.

5. Promoted regular knowledge sharing

I encourage writers to share product knowledge, lessons learned, useful practices, and approaches through monthly knowledge-sharing sessions.

Value: More informed work allocation, individual development opportunities, greater ownership, broader content formats, and stronger knowledge sharing across the team.

Case Study 01 · Customer Experience
Customer Experience Improvement

Enhanced Customer Experience Through Better Documentation Search

Customer surveys and internal feedback highlighted opportunities to improve how users searched for and discovered information in the technical documentation portal.

  • Investigated improvements within the existing platform and identified its limitations.
  • Evaluated alternative platforms against search, AI capabilities, integrations, usability, and stakeholder needs.
  • Coordinated platform selection and the migration of Help content with relevant stakeholders and content owners.
  • Helped introduce a platform with enhanced search capabilities and search analytics.
Value: Better information discovery and opportunities to use search insights for ongoing improvements.
Case Study 02 · AI Workflows
AI-Assisted Documentation

Built AI-Assisted Documentation Workflows

I helped shape AI-assisted workflows that support requirements analysis, preliminary drafting, and content refinement while following documentation standards.

  • Configured Gemini Gem instructions for writing style, approved product terminology, required sections, and author review.
  • Used Copilot and Gemini to summarise product information, analyse changes, draft content, and check clarity and consistency.
  • Used an Atlassian AI Agent to analyse Jira stories, subtasks, and linked issues for preliminary documentation impacts.
  • Helped team members understand how to structure instructions for the intended workflow.
Value: More structured AI support with clear review expectations and human accountability.
Case Study 03 · Analytics
Documentation Analytics

Used Documentation Analytics to Guide Content Improvement

I monitor documentation usage to understand which topics attract customer attention and to inform discussions about content needs.

  • Review page views and identify frequently accessed topics.
  • Share analytics during product meetings and with stakeholders.
  • Use high-demand topics to identify areas for further investigation and potential documentation gaps.
  • Bring usage patterns into content-prioritisation and resource-allocation discussions.
Value: Evidence-informed conversations about content priorities and customer needs.
Case Study 04 · Knowledge Management
AI-Powered Self-Service

Prepared Knowledge for AI-Powered Self-Service

I work with Support to identify customer pain points and prepare useful documentation knowledge for an AI-powered customer agent.

  • Identify frequently asked questions, use cases, and best practices with Support stakeholders.
  • Help ensure relevant product documentation is available as a knowledge source.
  • Support a knowledge approach in which scheduled indexing refreshes the source documentation.
  • Focus on content quality and knowledge readiness for customer self-service.

AI & Workflows

Leverage AI to Streamline Documentation Workflows

I help define practical AI-assisted workflows that support content analysis and drafting while retaining technical writers' responsibility for accuracy, context, and final approval.

AI-assisted content development

AI tools support selected tasks in the documentation lifecycle, guided by established writing standards and product context.

  • Requirements and change-request analysis
  • Preliminary content drafts
  • Content refinement and consistency checks
  • Identification of potential documentation impacts

Structured guidance and human review

Clear instructions help keep AI-assisted work aligned with product needs, audience expectations, and documentation standards.

  • Approved terminology and style guidance
  • Defined content structures and required sections
  • Prompt refinement and reusable instructions
  • Technical validation and author approval
Human oversight remains essential. AI supports analysis and drafting; technical writers validate product accuracy, context, terminology, and usability before content is approved.
