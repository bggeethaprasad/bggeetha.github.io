Case study 02 · AI-assisted workflows

Operationalising AI to Scale Documentation Excellence

Defined and implemented an AI-assisted documentation strategy that integrated product analysis, content creation, editorial review, and quality governance into a structured workflow. Established clear governance models that balanced AI-driven efficiency with human oversight, ensuring technical accuracy, content quality, and customer trust.

Transformed a largely manual documentation operation into a scalable, AI-enabled ecosystem that improved authoring efficiency, accelerated product and Jira analysis, and streamlined review cycles. By embedding AI into day-to-day documentation practices, the team increased responsiveness to documentation demands, improved content consistency, and created capacity for higher-value strategic and customer-focused work. The initiative positioned documentation as a more scalable function while maintaining rigorous standards for accuracy, quality, and accountability.

1. Analyse product changes

Use structured prompts to examine product information, Jira stories, linked issues, and relevant context to identify documentation implications and user needs.

2. Create structured first drafts

Use AI to develop initial content aligned with product terminology, documentation standards, and the intended user task.

3. Review and refine

Apply a separate review stage to assess completeness, structure, clarity, and alignment with product behaviour and documentation standards.

4. Retain human ownership

Writers validate technical details and resolve gaps with subject-matter experts. I review, edit, or write content directly when needed to maintain quality.

Approach: AI supports the workflow; it does not replace technical validation, editorial judgment, or writer accountability. Time savings have not been formally quantified.

How this improves the content operation

Faster drafting

A trained AI-assisted workflow gives writers a stronger starting draft, reducing manual first-draft effort and helping the team respond to documentation requests faster.

Consistency and review efficiency

Structured drafting and a dedicated review stage support more consistent structure, terminology, and documentation standards.

More efficient Jira analysis

AI agents help writers analyse Jira stories, linked issues, and product changes more efficiently.

Higher-value editorial work

Writers can spend more time validating technical details, improving quality, and designing content around customer needs.

These are qualitative operational outcomes; time savings and quality improvements have not yet been quantified.

Case study 03 · Strategic Content Refresh Program

Using Customer Insights to Drive Documentation Improvement

I led content modernization through the Strategic Content Refresh Program by generating a Top Viewed Topics report, assessing how old the content was, and sharing the findings with Product Managers and Engineering. Working with stakeholders, we identified priority topics, analyzed content gaps, refreshed the material, improved its visual presentation, and added use cases to every top-viewed topic.

Customer insights · Content quality · Analytics

Making content maintenance more intentional

My role: lead the Strategic Content Refresh Program, coordinate with Product Managers and Engineering, and guide evidence-based prioritization and content improvements.

The challenge

Older documentation needed a more systematic refresh, while the move to a new authoring platform created an opportunity to modernize the customer experience. Because the Document360 migration was completed only last month, the new platform is still too recent for meaningful performance conclusions.

What I did

  1. Built a prioritization report. Generated a Top Viewed Topics report and assessed content age.
  2. Aligned stakeholders. Shared the report with Product Managers and Engineering to agree on priority topics and content gaps.
  3. Refreshed content. Updated identified topics, improved visual presentation, and added use cases to all top-viewed topics.
  4. Supported the platform transition. Complemented the move from MindTouch to Document360.

Outcomes and next steps

  • Completed the move to Document360.
  • Used topic popularity and content age to guide refresh priorities.
  • Added use cases across all top-viewed topics and enhanced visual presentation.
  • Improved cross-functional alignment through collaboration with Product Managers and Engineering.
  • Established a basis for evaluating usage and customer feedback as more data becomes available.

How this supports customer success and efficiency

Customer success

Prioritizing high-traffic topics helps keep frequently used guidance current and relevant.

Self-service

Use cases and improved visual presentation make guidance more practical to navigate and apply.

Operational efficiency

Page-view evidence gives the team a repeatable way to prioritize maintenance.

Continuous improvement

The new platform provides a starting point for monitoring content usage and identifying improvements.

Customer conversations were positive, but measurable changes in support volume, satisfaction, or search success have not yet been established.

Measurement status: The migration is recent and platform metrics have not yet been evaluated. No quantified improvement is claimed at this stage.

Prepare knowledge for customer self-service

Case study 05 · Documentation leadership

Leading Documentation Strategy, Operations, and Team Development

I lead documentation strategy, operations, team development, and quality practices while aligning knowledge investments with product priorities and customer success goals. I identified opportunities for AI-assisted work, established a structured adoption approach, trained the team, and integrated the workflow into everyday documentation practices.

Function ownership · Team development · Cross-functional delivery

Connecting strategy, people, and execution

My role: own the Product Documentation function’s quality, maturity, and business impact while leading a team of four Product Documentation Specialists.

The leadership challenge

Documentation spans product changes, release commitments, customer questions, and ongoing content maintenance. The team needs clear priorities and consistent quality while requirements evolve.

How I lead

  1. Translate priorities into delivery. Connect business and product goals to documentation priorities, release notes, FAQs, and customer tasks.
  2. Develop the team and enable AI adoption. Identify AI opportunities, establish adoption practices, train writers, and set quality expectations. Coach writers to create goal-based, customer-first content.
  3. Stay close to the craft. Review, edit, and write content to resolve gaps and maintain editorial quality.
  4. Align cross-functional partners. Work with R&D, Technical Support, Services, and product stakeholders.

Leadership outcomes

  • Introduced AI-assisted workflows to reduce drafting effort.
  • Shifted documentation toward customer goals and outcomes.
  • Strengthened collaboration across Product, Support, and Engineering.
  • Established consistent documentation and review practices.
  • Balanced strategic direction, operational delivery, team development, and editorial leadership.

This player-coach approach keeps strategy connected to execution while maintaining human ownership of technical accuracy and final content quality.

Customer success

Operational efficiency

Clear priorities and coaching help coordinate work across releases, FAQs, maintenance, and stakeholder requests.

Consistent quality
