Case study 01 · Platform transformation

Enhance Technical Documentation Search with AI-Driven Capabilities

Initiated and coordinated the evaluation, selection, migration, and rollout of a new documentation authoring platform to address customer search needs and improve technical information discoverability.

Customer experience · Knowledge management · AI

Making technical information easier to find

Role: initiative owner and migration lead, coordinating platform evaluation and transition with content stakeholders.

The challenge

Customer surveys and internal stakeholder feedback highlighted opportunities to improve the search experience on our technical documentation portal. Customers needed a more effective way to discover relevant, accurate information without spending unnecessary time searching.

I first explored opportunities to enhance search within the existing platform. However, its functional limitations made it difficult to address the broader requirements, prompting me to initiate a systematic evaluation of alternative authoring platforms.

My approach

  1. Identified the problem through customer insights. Used customer survey responses and internal feedback to understand search-related challenges and identify opportunities to improve information discoverability.
  2. Evaluated alternative platforms systematically. Initiated an evaluation of multiple authoring platforms, comparing their fit against existing use cases, search capabilities, AI features, integrations, usability, and stakeholder requirements.
  3. Coordinated stakeholders and migration. Initiated and coordinated the platform transition, working with stakeholders responsible for maintaining content repositories and managing the migration of Help content alongside related content-improvement activities.
  4. Enabled AI-powered knowledge discovery. Transitioned to an AI-powered authoring platform with enhanced search capabilities and search analytics. These capabilities provide a foundation for helping customers find relevant information and understanding search behaviour to identify potential content gaps.

Value and future opportunities

  • Customer experience: Supports easier access to relevant technical information.
  • Data-informed content improvement: Search analytics can help reveal customer information needs and potential content gaps.
  • Content operations: Consolidates Help content on the selected authoring platform.
  • Continuous improvement: Creates opportunities to use search insights and customer feedback to guide documentation priorities.
Measurement status: The impact has not yet been formally measured. Future evaluation can examine search relevance, unsuccessful searches, frequently searched topics, customer feedback, and documentation-related support trends.

AI & documentation workflows

Applying AI across the content lifecycle

I help shape AI-assisted workflows that support technical writers from early requirement analysis through drafting and review. AI assists the process; writers validate technical accuracy and approve final content.

01. Requirement analysis

AI-assisted analysis of Jira stories, subtasks, and linked issues helps gather preliminary requirements and identify potential documentation impacts before writing begins.

02. Style-guided content drafting

Structured prompts, writing guidance, and defined content sections support first-draft creation aligned with documentation standards and the intended audience.

03. Review from multiple perspectives

Review workflows use criteria informed by Product Managers and Sales Engineers to help identify gaps in completeness, product context, and customer relevance.

Human accountability: These workflows support analysis, drafting, and review; technical writers remain responsible for validation, technical accuracy, and final approval. Efficiency gains have not been formally quantified.

Knowledge readiness · Customer self-service

Building a stronger knowledge foundation for AI-powered support

AI-powered customer experiences depend on useful, well-maintained source content. My contribution focuses on the documentation and knowledge practices that help make that source relevant to real customer needs.

Start with customer and Support needs

Work with Support to identify recurring customer pain points, frequently asked questions, practical use cases, and best practices that should be represented in the knowledge base.

Keep the knowledge source useful

Help prepare and maintain documentation that serves as a source for the customer-facing knowledge agent, with scheduled indexing refreshes supporting the availability of updated content.

Scope of contribution: My role centres on customer needs, documentation quality, and knowledge readiness—not engineering the agent, retrieval system, or underlying AI platform.

My approach

Good documentation is designed around what users need to do

I connect content quality with product understanding, customer feedback, team capability, and the systems that make knowledge maintainable and discoverable.

Start with user goals

Structure content around the tasks customers need to complete, the questions they ask, and the issues they need to resolve.

Use evidence to prioritise

Bring together customer feedback, support signals, and available analytics to identify where content or discovery can improve.

Scale quality through systems

Establish clear standards, information architecture, repeatable workflows, and review practices that help teams deliver consistent content.
