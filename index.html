Leadership Case Study

Strengthened Documentation Team Performance Through Data-Driven Leadership

Building team capability involves understanding individual strengths, balancing delivery needs, encouraging ownership, and creating opportunities for continuous learning.

Developing a capable, collaborative documentation team

Team management · Work allocation · Coaching · Quality · Knowledge sharing

Overview

I manage and develop a team of technical writers by combining delivery and workload information, documentation quality feedback, and individual observations. Through one-to-one discussions and ongoing coaching, I identify team strengths and development areas, allocate work thoughtfully, and encourage writers to take initiative, broaden their skills, and share knowledge.

1. Combined data with individual insights

I consider task delivery, workload, quality and review feedback, product knowledge, individual skills, learning progress, and documentation output when understanding team performance. I complement available data with observations and one-to-one discussions to understand individual strengths, challenges, and development opportunities.

2. Aligned work allocation with capabilities and growth

I consider workload, task complexity, product knowledge, and individual strengths when assigning work. I also encourage writers to develop new skills and take on unfamiliar challenges while balancing those opportunities with delivery commitments.

3. Encouraged initiative and ownership

I encourage team members to suggest improvements, explore better ways to communicate product information, and take ownership beyond routine assignments. Coaching and regular discussions help writers build confidence and contribute ideas to team practices.

4. Expanded content creation through videos

I create instructional videos and encourage writers to develop video-based content where visual demonstrations help explain product workflows and features. This gives the team additional ways to communicate complex information to users.

5. Promoted regular knowledge sharing

I encourage writers to share product knowledge, lessons learned, useful practices, and approaches through monthly knowledge-sharing sessions. These discussions help spread knowledge across the team and create opportunities for collaborative learning.

Value: A more informed approach to work allocation, clearer individual development opportunities, greater ownership, broader content formats, and stronger knowledge sharing across the team.

The approach combines available performance information with qualitative observations and coaching; no quantified improvement is claimed for this case study.

Built AI-Assisted Documentation Workflows

Workflow design · Prompt guidance · Content standards · Human review

Challenge

Product documentation depends on understanding product changes, requirements, approved terminology, content structure, and writing standards. AI assistance is most useful when it follows these requirements and fits the existing documentation process.

1. Configured a Gemini Gem for documentation

  • Defined standard writing style and guidance for legally approved product names and terminology.
  • Included relevant content artifacts, required content sections, and guidance for explaining the rationale behind new capabilities.
  • Established author review as a required part of the workflow.

2. Applied Copilot and Gemini to content development

  • Summarised product information and supported requirements analysis.
  • Drafted and refined content using defined prompts and guidance.
  • Reviewed clarity, structure, consistency, and alignment with writing standards.
  • Refined prompts to improve the usefulness of generated content.

3. Used an Atlassian AI Agent for Jira analysis

  • Supported analysis of Jira stories, subtasks, and linked issues.
  • Used the resulting analysis to prepare preliminary requirements summaries and identify potential documentation impacts.
  • Helped team members understand how to configure the agent and organise its instructions for the intended workflow.
Value: More structured support for requirements analysis and content development, with reusable writing guidance and clearer review expectations.

Time savings and productivity gains have not been formally quantified. AI-generated content remains subject to human validation and approval.
