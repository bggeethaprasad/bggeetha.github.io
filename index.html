Case Study 01 · Platform Transformation

Modernizing the Documentation Experience

I led the evaluation and transition to a new documentation platform to improve how customers discover technical information and establish a foundation for AI-enabled search and ongoing content improvement.

Customer Experience · Knowledge Management · AI

Making technical information easier to find

My role: documentation initiative owner and migration lead, aligning platform decisions with customer needs and business priorities.

The challenge

Customer surveys and stakeholder feedback highlighted opportunities to improve the search experience on the technical documentation portal. Customers needed a more effective way to discover relevant, accurate information.

My approach

  1. Started with customer insights. Used survey responses and internal feedback to understand search-related challenges and opportunities to improve information discoverability.
  2. Evaluated alternatives systematically. Assessed authoring platforms against practical use cases, search capabilities, AI features, integrations, usability, and stakeholder requirements.
  3. Connected platform decisions to business needs. Considered customer experience, content operations, usability, and the practical work involved in migrating and maintaining Help content.
  4. Established a foundation for AI-enabled discovery. Transitioned to Document360, with enhanced search capabilities and analytics that can help surface information needs and potential content gaps.

Value and opportunities

  • Customer experience: Supports easier access to relevant technical information.
  • Data-informed improvement: Search insights can help identify information needs and potential content gaps.
  • Content operations: Consolidates Help content on the selected authoring platform.
  • Continuous improvement: Creates opportunities to use search insights and customer feedback to guide documentation priorities.
Measurement status: The impact has not yet been formally measured. Future evaluation can examine search relevance, unsuccessful searches, frequently searched topics, customer feedback, and documentation-related support trends.

Case Study 02 · AI-Assisted Workflows

Building a Structured AI-Assisted Documentation Workflow

I designed a repeatable Gemini workflow that supports product analysis, first-draft development, and content review. My role combines workflow design and guidance for writers with human editorial oversight and accountability for quality.

1. Analyse product changes

Use structured prompts to examine product information, Jira stories, linked issues, and relevant context to identify documentation implications and user needs.

2. Develop a first draft

Guide AI-assisted drafting using product terminology, content standards, and the intended user task to create a starting point for writers.

3. Review and refine

Apply a separate review stage to assess completeness, structure, clarity, and alignment with product behaviour and documentation standards.

4. Retain human ownership

Writers validate technical details and resolve gaps with subject-matter experts. I review, edit, or write content directly when needed to maintain quality.

Approach: AI supports the workflow; it does not replace technical validation, editorial judgment, or writer accountability. Time savings have not been formally quantified.

Case Study 03 · Content Quality and Insights

Turning Customer Insights into Better Documentation

I use customer feedback, content reviews, and available analytics to identify information needs and guide documentation improvements. The Go Green initiative focused on improving the currency and usefulness of content, particularly topics attracting customer attention.

Identify what needs improvement

Bring together customer feedback, support questions, documentation surveys, and available page-view analytics to identify outdated, high-interest, or potentially incomplete topics.

Prioritize around customer needs

Use findings to guide topic updates, strengthen content structure, improve task-based explanations, and align documentation with current product behaviour.

Build a repeatable improvement process

Use content reviews and analytics deliverables to make documentation maintenance more systematic rather than relying only on release-driven updates.

Keep content connected to product priorities

Coordinate with stakeholders so documentation improvements reflect customer needs, product changes, and the information users need to succeed.

Evidence: The initiative was informed by customer feedback and available analytics. Specific business outcomes should be reported only where verified and appropriately attributable.

Case Study 04 · Conversational AI Knowledge

Preparing Product Knowledge for Conversational AI

Effective conversational AI depends on relevant, clear, and useful knowledge. My contribution focuses on helping Support translate customer questions and practical troubleshooting experience into knowledge that can support a customer-facing AI assistant.

Represent real customer questions

Worked with Support to develop questions and answers grounded in customer needs, common scenarios, and the information users need to understand and use the product.

Capture practical troubleshooting guidance

Helped develop best practices and troubleshooting questions that make product knowledge more useful for resolving common customer problems.

Connect documentation with self-service

Applied documentation and knowledge-management judgment to help make product information more relevant to a conversational support experience.

Keep the contribution grounded

Focused on the content and knowledge side of the experience rather than claiming ownership of the AI assistant's engineering, retrieval system, or underlying platform.
