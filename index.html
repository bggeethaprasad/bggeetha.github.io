My perspective

Good content starts with understanding the problem.

Creating documentation is only part of the challenge. We need to understand what people are trying to do, organise information around their needs, make it discoverable, and keep it useful as products evolve.

That means listening to customers, working closely with product and engineering teams, and treating content quality as an ongoing responsibility.

AI opens up new possibilities for requirement analysis, drafting and review. The opportunity is not simply to produce more content, but to improve the quality, consistency and usefulness of the knowledge we deliver.
